About ur/gd Studios

We build the tools we wish we had — calm, respectful software for people who need tools that work.

Who we are

ur/gd Studios is a creative technology studio. We design and build web applications for people navigating complex or overwhelming situations — whether that's managing community communications, preserving memories, or creating something tangible from a digital file.

We start with the belief that software should be stable, compassionate, and built to last. If a tool doesn't leave you better than it found you, it's not done. That applies to every line of code, every screen, and every word we write.

Everything we build lives under one promise: your data, your privacy, your experience. You're good.

Three Studios

ur/gd operates through three studios, each with a distinct purpose and expression. Together, they form a system that builds the backbone, delivers the work, and creates the tools.

Vault

Purpose: Internal tools, frameworks, templates, standards

Expression: Precision — builds the backbone

Vault is where we build the infrastructure that makes everything else possible. Design systems, deployment pipelines, security frameworks, architectural standards — the quiet machinery that ensures quality and consistency across all ur/gd work.

Station

Purpose: Client collaboration, project delivery

Expression: Trust — mission control for joint work

Station is where we partner with clients to build tools tailored to their needs. This is mission control: scoping, communication, delivery, and iteration. Every project is a collaboration, and Station is where that work happens.

Orbit

Purpose: ur/gd's own apps (Broadcast, Memento, Stitch, etc.)

Expression: Humanity — personal, calm, human tools

Orbit is where we build our own applications. These are the tools we wish we had — designed for real people, in real situations. No growth hacking, no dark patterns, no noise. Just software that respects your time and attention.

Guiding Principles

These aren't slogans. They're how we make decisions — every feature, every interaction, every line of code.

Stability

Reliability and trust in our tools. Systems stay up, data stays safe, and features work as expected. We don't ship fast and break things — we ship when it's ready, and it works.

Compassion

Empathy for the user's context and needs. We design for people who are overwhelmed, not people with infinite time. Error messages are calm. Guidance is clear. The interface doesn't add stress — it reduces it.

Connection

Helping people feel grounded and linked. Whether that's connecting a community through SMS, preserving a memory through a photo, or linking a digital design to a physical creation — our tools create bridges, not walls.

Growth

Personal, technical, and community advancement. We build systems that grow with you. They adapt, they improve, and they don't lock you into patterns that stop working when your needs change.

Warmth

Experiences that feel human, not mechanical. Tone matters. Design matters. The way a button responds to a click, the language in a confirmation message, the spacing around text — warmth is in the details, and we care about all of them.

North Star

We build the tools we wish we had. That means starting from the need, not the tech. It means asking "does this make someone's life better?" before asking "can we build it?" It means prioritizing clarity over cleverness, and trust over growth metrics. If a tool works the way it should, you don't think about the tool — you think about what you're making with it. That's the goal.

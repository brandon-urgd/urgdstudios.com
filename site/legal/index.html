3. Application Transparency

Each ur/gd application reflects the same design principles, with functionality-specific data practices.

Broadcast Philosophy: Broadcast is built on the principle of “One voice, many listeners.” We believe in respectful broadcasting that gives everyone control. It is designed to eliminate the noise of group chats by allowing senders to speak once, while giving listeners the absolute power to pause (“QUIET”) or step back (“STOP”) without social friction, drama, or guilt. We do not support two-way chat or complex marketing automations that treat listeners as data points rather than people.
